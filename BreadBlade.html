About this game

Bread & Blade is a mobile strategy game set in the nomadic Turkic / Seljuk world — a hybrid of idle production and wave defense. The player builds a village, runs production chains, trains soldiers and defends the walls against sieges that arrive at regular intervals. The design principle fits in one line: progression is slow, tempo is high.

There is plenty to do inside a session — fill queues, collect goods, line up soldiers, hold off the wave — yet levelling up takes days. The goal is to leave every session feeling it was time well spent, while still leaving a reason to come back tomorrow. The target session is 10–15 minutes, two or three times a day; the offline earnings cap is 8 hours.

Bread & Blade, göçebe Türk / Selçuklu dünyasında geçen, idle üretim + dalga savunması hibriti bir mobil strateji oyunu. Oyuncu bir köy kurar, üretim zincirlerini işletir, asker yetiştirir ve düzenli aralıklarla gelen kuşatmalara karşı surlarını savunur. Tasarım ilkesi tek cümle: ilerleme yavaş, tempo yüksek.

Oturum içinde yapacak iş boldur — kuyruk doldur, ürün topla, asker diz, dalgayı savun — ama seviye atlamak günler alır. Hedef, her oturumdan "dolu dolu geçti" hissiyle çıkmak ama buna rağmen ertesi güne bir hedef bırakmak. Oturum hedefi 10–15 dakika, günde 2–3 kez; çevrimdışı kazanç tavanı 8 saat.

Production is not automatic. Tap a building, drag a recipe into a slot, the inputs are deducted immediately and the timer starts; the finished product drops in front of the building as a real prop and is collected by tapping it. Collection is kept manual on purpose, because that is the player's actual action during a session. Waves also arrive only when the player starts them; during a wave buying, repairing and selling are locked — only unit command stays open.

On the economy side two rules decide everything. First: prices are written in time, not numbers — as "how many minutes of production" — and no price may exceed 60% of the store that holds it. The one time this rule was broken, going from keep level 1 to 2 became mathematically impossible. Second: diamonds cannot buy resources. Diamonds only finish pending work and open permanent production slots. Gold comes only from waves, so growing the village depends on fighting.

The prototype counts as finished when four things hold at once: the core functions are complete; every building has its L1 and L2 modelled; balance is settled; and the three systems inside the scope gate (builder's hut, households / troop cap, villager hut with wheat-and-bread production) are included. Characters, new unit types and animations sit outside that gate — deferred.

That is why the screenshots are shown blurred for now: the scenes are still at whitebox blocking stage and the art pass has not happened. The art direction is Clash of Clans style — chamfered hard edges, a saturated palette, deep AO — and the Seljuk / nomadic Turkic identity is structural rather than decorative: felt, lashed poles, guy ropes, wicker fencing, and at higher levels turquoise tilework and kümbet forms.

Üretim otomatik değil. Binaya tıkla, tarifi yuvaya sürükle, girdi anında düşer ve süre işlemeye başlar; biten ürün binanın önüne gerçek bir prop olarak düşer ve tıklanıp toplanır. Toplama bilinçli olarak manuel tutuldu, çünkü oyuncunun oturumdaki asıl eylemi bu. Dalgalar da yalnız oyuncu başlattığında geliyor; dalga sırasında al/onar/sat kapalı, yalnız birim komutası açık.

Ekonomi tarafında iki kural her şeyi belirliyor. Birincisi: fiyatlar sayıyla değil süreyle yazılıyor — "kaç dakikalık üretim" karşılığı — ve hiçbir fiyat, onu tutan deponun %60'ını geçemiyor. Bu kural bir kez ihlal edildiğinde kale seviye 1'den 2'ye geçiş matematiksel olarak imkânsız hale gelmişti. İkincisi: elmas kaynak satın alamıyor. Elmas yalnız bekleyen işi bitiriyor ve kalıcı üretim yuvası açıyor. Altın da yalnız dalgadan geliyor, yani köyü büyütmek savaşmaya bağlı.

Prototip şu dördü birden sağlandığında bitmiş sayılacak: temel fonksiyonlar tamam; her binanın L1 ve L2 hâli modellenmiş; denge sağlanmış; ve kapsam kapısındaki üç sistem (inşaatçı evi, hane/asker tavanı, köylü evi + buğday-ekmek üretimi) dahil edilmiş. Karakterler, yeni birim tipleri ve animasyonlar bu kapının dışında — sonraya bırakıldı.

Görseller bu yüzden şimdilik bulanık gösteriliyor: sahneler whitebox bloklama aşamasında, nihai sanat geçişi yapılmadı. Sanat yönü Clash of Clans stilinde — sert kenarlara pah, doygun palet, derin AO — ve Selçuklu / göçebe Türk kimliği dekor değil yapısal: keçe, bağlanmış sırık, gergi ipi, hasır çit, üst seviyelerde firuze çini ve kümbet formu.

Manual production chain
Wheat → flour → bread, stone → steel. Two separate stores: a granary for raw materials and a depot for goods; capacity comes from the keep level (50 / 100 / 180 / 300 / 500). Steel is a deliberate bottleneck — heavy armoured troops and the upper keep upgrade both go through it.
10 resources Granary / Depot Hay Day pacing
⚔️
Wave defense and squads
The wave is split into squads that spawn at a fixed enemy camp and march in block formation. Enemy types unlock slowly: archers at wave 3, heavy armour at wave 6. The ramp is hand-written — 3/4/5/6/7/9/10/12, then 8% scaling per wave, capped at 50.
Squad formation Hand-written ramp Player-triggered
🧱
Walls are consumable
When a wave ends the rubble is swept and the wall is rebuilt for 6 stone. A breach opens a real corridor in the navmesh; a modular tile system picks the piece and yaw from its neighbours. Walls, towers and traps skip the builder queue — they are between-wave preparation decisions.
Modular tiles Navmesh breach Consumable
🧭
The lever that kills the maze exploit
Each enemy decides once per life: if the navmesh path is shorter than 1.75× the straight-line distance it walks through the opening to the base, otherwise it switches to siege. That kills the "build a maze and walk the enemy forever" exploit.
One-time decision ×1.75 threshold Balance lever
🏠
Villagers and troops share one pool
Every household carries a population of two, and villagers occupy that space too — creating a real trade-off between growing the village and growing the army. While the player is away, villagers produce just enough wheat and bread to field militia; even after a day with no login, some resistance has accumulated.
Household system Troop cap Offline production
💎
Diamonds cannot buy resources
Diamonds do exactly two things: finish pending work and open a permanent production slot (10 → 20 → 40, at most 3). The moment they can buy resources, the village stops being the source of anything. The prototype has no purchase screen; diamonds come only from wave rewards.
