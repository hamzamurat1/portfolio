ABOUT PROJECTS ERZURUM
THE LAST FRONT

Murat's
Village Home

A village home in 1920s Erzurum. Murat lives peacefully with his wife and children — the war is still far away.

CHAPTER I — AWAKENING

Peace
Turns to Front Line

The sky darkens. When Murat opens his eyes he finds himself in the trenches, defending against enemy waves.

CHAPTER II — TO ARMS

At the
Training Camp

Time rewinds. Murat's military training and his first meeting with Hüseyin — the foundations of their comradeship are laid here.

MOTIVATION

A Homeland
Worth Defending

As enemy forces push deeper into Anatolia, only one thing keeps Murat at the front: his family and his village.

CHAPTER III — STEEL STORM

An Abandoned
Tank

"Can we get this running?" Murat and Hüseyin tear apart the enemy's logistics lines with a captured tank.

CHAPTER IV — DARK OPERATION

A Silent
Raid

At night, they infiltrate the captured enemy headquarters. The goal: seize the secret war documents and withdraw unnoticed.

EPILOGUE

The Last Mission
at Aziziye Fort

Written around Hüseyin's sacrifice, this story is in development in Unreal Engine 5 as Erzurum: The Last Front. A single-player narrative experience of 6–8 hours.
